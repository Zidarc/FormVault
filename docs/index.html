FormVault Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version: FormVault has no server. Your vault lives in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the AI provider you choose, with your own key, and only when you ask for it.

What FormVault stores

Your vault (profile, projects, hackathons, experience, achievements, skills, saved answers and writing-style rules), your settings and your AI key. All of it is kept in your browser's extension storage on your computer. FormVault's developers never receive it.

Free AI tiers

On Google's free Gemini tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products and human reviewers may read them. This is why FormVault never sends your personal details to the AI. If that matters to you, use a paid key, or Chrome's on-device model where available. Users in the EU, Switzerland and the UK need a paid Gemini key.

Images

Images you add (screenshots, certificate photos, scanned PDFs) are read with OCR on your computer. Only the text it finds, with emails, phone numbers, ID numbers and your name removed, is sent to your AI provider. If OCR can't read an image at all, FormVault sends a smaller copy of that one image to your AI provider instead. When you right-click an image and choose Add image to FormVault, FormVault takes a one-time capture of the visible part of that tab and crops it to the image. This only happens on that click.

Reading web pages

FormVault reads a form only when you click its icon (or press its shortcut) on that page. On LinkedIn, AI-chat sites, Gmail, Outlook, Credly and Coursera it may show a small "Add to vault?" chip; deciding whether to show it is a keyword check done on your computer. The text is only sent to your AI provider if you tap the chip. If you paste a link into Quick add, FormVault opens that one page (after asking for access to that site) and reads its text. FormVault never reads pages in the background and never presses Submit.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no cookies of our own, no selling or sharing of data. There is nothing to sell — we don't have it.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete my vault" erases it. Uninstalling the extension erases everything it stored. If you used Drive sync, you can also remove FormVault's app data from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Open source

You can check all of this in the code: github.com/Zidarc/FormVault. Questions? Open an issue there.
